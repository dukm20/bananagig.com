# PRD v9.4 reconciliation with the PRD/TRP alignment

The product requirements document (`BananaGig_PRD.docx`, v9.4, 5 October 2026) is a Word file kept locally outside git (`docs/design/` is excluded), so it is not edited by repository checkpoints. The canonical, versioned statement of product direction is [`BananaGig_PRD_TRP_Alignment.md`](BananaGig_PRD_TRP_Alignment.md) (PRD-TRP-001); the canonical plan is [`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md). **Where the PRD text below differs from the alignment document, the alignment document wins** until the owner revises the PRD. This table is the list for whoever updates the Word file.

The PRD's numbered requirements (CU-, PR-, SV-, AD-, R-, NF-) stay valid as detailed specifications; only the framing, priority and ordering below change.

| # | PRD v9.4 says | Canonical direction now | Effect on engineering |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2. Product Vision: BananaGig is "a local-services lead and subscription platform for discrete bookable work"; the differentiator is "local demand aggregation". | BananaGig is a **neighborhood demand-bundling marketplace**. BananaDrop (provider-controlled neighborhood group pricing) is the defining product mechanism. | Product, UI and engineering framing: lead with the neighborhood price loop, not the billing model. |
| 2 | 17 Delivery Phases: BananaDrop is **P2**, build step 8, after "Release 1 = all P1 rows" (build steps 1 to 7). | BananaDrop is a **first-class core marketplace domain**, not a later phase. The MVP is not complete until the group-pricing loop works end to end. | The roadmap moves the DROP domain checkpoints before search and before the customer booking experience (ROADMAP section 3). Release 1 as defined in PRD 17.1 is no longer the MVP bar. |
| 3 | 6 Build order: Supply (3), Discovery (4), Booking and payment (5), ... BananaDrop (8). | Provider and supply foundation, then DROP-001 to DROP-010, then SEARCH, UI-BASE-001, customer experience, booking, then DROP-011 to DROP-016. | Search is BananaDrop-aware (ranking, eligibility, density signals); booking is BananaDrop-aware (qualification, participation, protected price). |
| 4 | 2 and 5: "Fixed Gig + **optional** Drop tiers"; BananaDrop is "the commercial campaign" (an add-on to a Gig). | A customer books at the current bookable price; future tiers are conditional; eligible existing bookings get later reductions only under explicit price-protection rules. The provider controls services, area, capacity, windows, deadline, tiers and floor. | Gig, capacity and BananaDrop stay three independent objects (PRD 5.2 stands); BananaDrop state is authoritative on the platform; clients never compute group progress or a protected price. |
| 5 | CU-04 and 18: the customer authorises "the maximum disclosed amount" and is "captured at the **lower locked price**" (price lock before fulfilment). | "Price protection": the customer books at the current price; later tier unlocks reduce the price of eligible existing bookings according to explicit, auditable protection rules; there is no silent repricing. | The price-lock vs price-protection mechanics (lock time, final price, adjustment history, idempotency) are decided at DROP-008 and DROP-013 against the owner's rules; do not assume either until then. |
| 6 | Throughout (product vision, billing and plan sections): provider subscriptions, the prepaid wallet and Banana Credits are presented as the platform billing model and as a leading business mechanism. | **Banana Credits are a provider monetization mechanism, not the main customer-facing value proposition**; they are supporting, secondary to the BananaDrop story. | Customer-facing copy and UI never lead with credits. Billing checkpoints keep their place in the later roadmap blocks. |
| 7 | PR-03.08: variants and add-ons "can be marked **Drop-eligible** or not". | Compatible services may contribute to one BananaDrop and keep different base prices and ladders; one BananaDrop is never one universal price. | The eligibility mapping (which Gigs and variants take part) belongs to DROP-002, keyed to the exact Gig version and variant key; CAT-004 stores no Drop flag and no tier on a variant. |
| 8 | 5.2 Three independent objects: Gig, Capacity, BananaDrop; "No feature may couple these". | Unchanged, and now reinforced: additional customer demand never automatically creates capacity; provider capacity, time and resources stay authoritative. | No change. |

Items the alignment document does not decide and that stay with the owner (PRD open questions): the default price-lock time before a BananaDrop fulfilment window, the split of late-cancellation fees (Q32), and every business value (all remain configuration, never code).
